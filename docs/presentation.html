Context

The problem it solves

Today at NovaRetail

150 stores · 8 warehouses · 300+ suppliers · ~5,000 shipments a day. When a shipment is late, stock runs short, or a supplier fails, ops jump across separate portals and coordinate recovery by hand.

Purpose of the assistant

One place to ask in plain English — understand the incident, route to the right supply-chain function, pull live facts, and propose a recovery plan with a human still in control of writes.

What ops can do

  • Track shipments and quantify delay impact
  • Check stock, shortages, and transfer options
  • Find and compare alternative suppliers
  • Raise incidents, escalate, and plan recovery

What it opens for NovaRetail

  • Faster disruption response across teams
  • Unified visibility instead of portal-hopping
  • AI-assisted recovery with approval gates
  • Less manual coordination on every incident

Delivery · TM1

Andriy Franko

Intake, incidents & the ops console

What he built

  • Request Intake Agent — classify intent, normalise IDs, validate against data, carry entity memory
  • Incident Analysis Agent — facts, damage assessment, rule-based severity, duplicate check
  • Streamlit chat UI — conversations, workflow trace, export
  • Memory & history — SQLite checkpointer so threads and approvals survive restarts

Why it matters

  • Ops type shp 2026 2 — intake turns it into SHP-2026-0002
  • “That shipment” resolves from conversation memory on later turns
  • Severity stays a rule engine, not model guesswork
  • HITL in the UI — Approve executes the write; Reject changes nothing

Delivery · TM3

Pavlo Lohvinov

Inventory, supplier & the handoff

His agents & tools

  • Inventory — stock, shortages, required qty, transfers (6 tools)
  • Supplier — availability, alternatives, compare, landed cost (6 tools)
  • Invariants: available = on_hand − reserved; transfers never breach donor safety stock
  • Suspended suppliers are never offered as available

Cross-agent handoff

  • Inventory emits remaining_gap / fully_covered
  • Supplier uses that gap as buy quantity — or says missing quantity — cannot cost
  • Recovery prefers transfer when covered; otherwise the recommended supplier + landed cost
  • Read-only workers — writes stay behind the approval gate

His job in the loop: turn “we’re short” into a cover plan the recovery step can act on.
